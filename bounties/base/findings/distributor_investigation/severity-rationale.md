# Severity Rationale: Coinbase Echo Distributor (F-003)

## 1. Classification Standard
Evaluated against Cantina / Immunefi standard smart-contract vulnerability taxonomy:
- **Recommended Rating:** **HIGH**
- **Impact Dimension:** Direct, permanent loss of earned performance fees (carry) and forced premature liquidation of carry pools. Does not permit catastrophic draining of other users' principal capital, correctly bounding it below Critical.
- **Likelihood Dimension:** High. Exploitation requires zero compromised keys, zero administrative roles, and zero off-chain or transaction ordering manipulation. Any authorized investor with an unexpired voucher can execute replay via standard Web3 transactions. Any third party can replay `withdrawCarry` directly from mempool observation.

## 2. Distinction Between Gross Claim and Net Loss
- Gross token claims represent legitimate ownership rights governed by `distributionShares`.
- Net financial loss is strictly the performance fee (`amountCarry`) that is avoided by claimants and lost by deal leads.
- In all tested scenarios, 100% of carry generated by the replayed tranche is permanently evaded.
