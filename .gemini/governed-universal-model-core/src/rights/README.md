# Intellectual-property and revenue governance module

This module tracks creator assertions, provenance references, permitted/excluded uses, documented grants, and contract-grounded revenue allocation records for assets managed by your own application.

## Important boundaries
- A registry entry is not a legal determination of copyright, patentability, ownership, inventorship, trade-secret status, or enforceability.
- Creator assertions should be supported with timestamped drafts, version history, source files, signed agreements, and counsel review where material.
- An opt-in to model training is not, by itself, evidence of a revenue-sharing promise. Do not create a payable allocation without a documented agreement that specifies the relevant asset, parties, scope, rate/basis, and conditions.
- Do not enter private third-party conversations or personal data into this registry unless there is a lawful basis and authorization to do so.
- The module records agreements; it does not collect money, determine damages, send invoices, or provide legal advice.

## Suggested commercial workflow
1. Preserve provenance for your own work (dated commits, design docs, releases, and contribution records).
2. Identify which assets are yours, jointly created, licensed, assigned, or uncertain.
3. Have an attorney review the applicable terms and any agreement before asserting a compensation right.
4. Negotiate a written license, royalty, assignment, or other commercial agreement with a willing counterparty.
5. Record executed agreement references and use contract-defined calculations only.
